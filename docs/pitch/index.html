Minsky: a chat assistant that takes a bank customer's dispute from "I don't recognize this charge" to an open case

It finds the charge in the customer's account, checks it against the bank's dispute rules, and then opens the case, explains why there is nothing to dispute, or passes it to a person with the facts already collected. Every case lands in a back-office queue ordered by urgency. People still decide every dispute, and the system never moves money.

Built on the LATAM Bank dataset: 150k customers in Mexico, Colombia and Argentina, three years of data.

Why this split

  • Free-text Spanish with typos is the one thing code handles badly, so that is the only job of the model.
  • Who the customer is, which records they can see, and whether a charge can be disputed are decided by code that we test, and every decision cites the rule that made it.
  • The system only tells the customer an action happened after reading it back from the database.

What it will not do

  • Show another customer's transactions, even if asked for one by id
  • Grant a refund because "the manager approved it"
  • Follow instructions hidden in the message
  • Act without an explicit "yes" from the customer

What it is worth

Most disputes would not need an agent to get started

If customers disputed their recent charges

We ran the bank's dispute rules on every charge from the last 120 days of data (425,209 charges) and counted where each one would end estimate

54 % case opened
8 %
38 % to a person

Green: opened in the chat · cyan: nothing to dispute · amber: fraud, above USD 500, repeat complaint

So 62 % never need an agent at intake. For card purchases, the most common thing to dispute, it is 96 %. Almost every case sent to a person is a transfer or payment above USD 500.

The USD 500 limit is the bank's choice of risk. At USD 2,000, the share without an agent rises to 80 %.

For the customer

An open case in three messages, at any hour, instead of a phone queue and a 7-minute call that ends without a solution more than half the time.

1.4-1.8 s of system time per conversation in live tests measured

For the bank

About 1,600 agent hours a year go to dispute calls today estimate. Fraud cases get the card blocked in the first minute.

The back office gets the triage it lacks: fraud first (within 4 hours), then high amounts, each case with the facts and open questions ready. Investigating and deciding stays with people.

Estimates assume any recent charge is equally likely to be disputed, and that disputes are 40 % of complaint calls as they are of complaints. "It wasn't me" claims also go to fraud and are not counted.5 / 6

A first pilot

Unrecognized card charges in one country, running next to the current agents for four weeks. Compare the share opened without an agent and the time to an open case against today.

Limits we state openly

The data is synthetic and has no dispute conversations, so the rules are a policy we wrote, not learned from history. There is no Portuguese source data. The language model runs outside AWS for now because our account cannot use Bedrock yet.
