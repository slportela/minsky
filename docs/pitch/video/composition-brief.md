# Composition brief: Minsky pitch video

- **Composition:** `composition/` (generated by `build.py` from `scenes/` and `index.template.html`; do not hand-edit `composition/index.html` or `composition/compositions/`).
- **Output:** `brag.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps), poster `brag.jpg`.
- **Structure:** one sub-composition per scene (`s0`…`s8`), each with its own paused GSAP timeline; the root mounts them in sequence. Shared CSS/JS (phone mockup, chat playback, count-ups) is inlined by `build.py` from `scenes/shared/`.
- **Timing:** scene length = max(designed minimum, last voice clip end + 1 s). Captions are timed proportionally inside each voice clip. Music volume is an automation lane: duck under voice, silence before the logo, fade-out.
- **Assets:** GSAP bundled locally (`assets/lib/`), voice clips `assets/vo/`, music `assets/music/`, CC0 SFX from the brag library `assets/sfx/`, synthesized buzz.
- **Source material:** chat structure from the real `/chat` (customer/assistant bubbles, transaction card with masked card, Yes/No), console structure from the real `/console` (queue summary, triage-ordered list, case detail with verified facts, customer claim, actions, open questions, claim button). Conversations from the deck, translated to English.
- **Gate:** `hyperframes check` with zero errors, then snapshots at scene midpoints, then render.
